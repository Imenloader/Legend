IMMORTAL LIFE CODEX: COMPREHENSIVE ARCHITECTURAL PLAN
======================================================

1. BIRTH & BACKGROUND SYSTEM
Players begin in the womb. Background is rolled based on previous Karma.
- Poor: High XP mult, 0 Gold.
- Rich: Low XP mult, 10,000 Gold.
- Royal: Massive base stats, high Karma debt.

2. FAMILY & RELATIONS
- Procedural generation of parents and siblings.
- Marriage system with NPC bonding and children.

3. GOLDEN FINGER CHEAT SYSTEMS
- Many Children Many Blessings: descendant-based stat scaling.
- Killing System: Kill-based permanent Atk gain.
- Sword Immortal: 2x Sword damage, auto-mastery.

4. SECT MANAGEMENT
- Join existing sects with rank progression.
- Found your own sect at Level 5.
- Recruit and train disciples.
